Important Instructions

  • Each question carries 1 mark.
  • There is no negative marking.
  • You will receive 2 minutes per question.
  • Your exam timer starts when you start the exam.
  • Choose the best answer for each question.
  • Submit your answers before the timer expires.
  • Tab switches and loss of focus may show a browser warning.
  • Text selection, dragging, and copy, cut, or paste actions in the exam area are discouraged or blocked where supported; clipboard actions may show a warning.
  • You may enter fullscreen using the control on the exam page. Fullscreen is unavailable in some browsers.
  • Monitoring alerts are confirmed by the server, which applies the existing warning and disqualification policy.
